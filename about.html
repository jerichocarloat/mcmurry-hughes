About · McMurry & Hughes

Both sides of the desk.

McMurry & Hughes is Tino Langner's company, based in San José, Costa Rica. We recruit for employers, and we equip recruiting firms.

San José · Costa Rica Hiring for Germany · Europe · North America
01Why usFour things we can stand behind

We have seen hiring from the inside.

Almost everyone in a city at dusk is working somewhere. Almost none of them are looking. Reaching them is a different job from advertising a role, and it is the one we do.

Why employers work with us
01We start where your search stopsWe look at the market before we approach anyone, so your shortlist arrives with the market it came from. We look past job titles too: the right person often calls the job something else.
02We know how recruiting runsWe set up and support the systems recruiting firms work in, clean their data and do their research. That work shows us where searches run out, and where the people they miss tend to be.
03The person you write to stays with youTino Langner reads your first message and stays your contact from the first reply to the introduction. You are not passed to someone you have never spoken to.
04We say what we do not knowEvery figure we show has a date and a base. Our largest client sits next to our record, not in a footnote. We would rather lose a pitch than win it on a number we cannot show you.
02The groupOne owner · each business keeps its own clients

One company, three businesses.

They were not designed as one system. Each started because a client needed something done. They share an owner, a place and a way of treating data.
