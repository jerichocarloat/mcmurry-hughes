01 The problem What a usual search misses

No company sees the whole market.

Most companies hire from the part of the market they already know how to reach: their own city, their usual job boards, an agency they have used before, a salary band set a few years ago. Nobody decided that boundary. It grew over time.

For most roles this works. For a hard role, the right person is often just outside that boundary, so the search keeps coming back with the same few names.

02 How it works Research · find · interview · introduce

We recruit outside the market you already search.

We work on one role at a time, in three stages. You see what we found before you decide anything.

  1. Where they are
    01

    We research the market

    We find out where people who could do your role actually work, including cities, countries and language groups you do not usually search. We count them, and we write down what we could not find out.

  2. Counted · dated6
    02

    We send you a market map

    A market map is a short, dated document about one role. It shows how many people could do the job outside your usual search, where they are, and what we do not know. We do not charge for it, and you keep it whether or not you work with us.

  3. Introduced, with consent
    03

    We find, interview and introduce

    If you want to go ahead, we contact those people ourselves, interview them against your standard in the language the role needs, and introduce the ones worth meeting, with a list of who we ruled out and why.

03 Who we can help Five conditions

We are useful to a narrow kind of company.

All five need to be true. If the last one is not true yet, we cannot help with this role, and we would rather tell you here than after weeks of searching. You are still welcome to write: we will tell you what would need to change.

Read the full fit check
The five conditions
01You hire repeatedlyThe same or similar roles, over a year or more.
02You have a clear standardYou would rather leave a role open than hire the wrong person.
03Your own market is small for the roleLocation, language, sector or pay leaves only a few people near you who could do it.
04You have no large recruiting teamHiring is often run by founders and line managers.
05You can employ someone who is not localThis one is essential. You need a company entity, an employer of record or a contractor arrangement already in place. We do not provide any of these.
