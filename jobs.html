How applying works

  1. 01

    You apply by email

    The form prepares an email with your answers. You attach your CV and send it from your own email program.

  2. 02

    We read it

    Your email goes to Tino Langner. If your experience could fit a role, we arrange a conversation.

  3. 03

    Shortlist

    If you are one of the strongest people for a role, we describe you to the employer in our own words.

  4. 04

    You meet the employer

    Only with your agreement. Then you talk to the company directly.

What happens to your details

Your application is an email to tino@mcmurry-hughes.com. This website does not store it. We do not publish names, photographs or current employers on this site, and we ask you before we introduce you to any company.

Hearing back

We aim to answer every application, including when the answer is no. If you have not heard from us, write to tino@mcmurry-hughes.com.
