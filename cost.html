What it suggests

Answer the questions about applicants, employment and remote status to see a reading.

Your search may be the constraint, not the role.

Few qualified applicants, and you can employ people outside your country. That usually means the people exist, but not where you have been looking. A market snapshot shows who is outside your search: one page for this role, free, before you commit.

You hire this kind of role several times a year. That is the pattern the desk is built for.

See where else you could search

Your answers go with you.

Your issue may be selection, offer or process rather than supply.

Three or more qualified applicants in a month means the market is reaching you. A wider search would add names, not solve the problem. Look first at how candidates are assessed, what the offer says and how long the process takes.

Read how we work anyway

A local recruiter may be the better fit.

The role is on-site and you cannot employ anyone outside your country. We recruit in markets beyond the usual search, so for this role a recruiter who knows your city will serve you better. We would rather say so now.

The people may exist, but you cannot hire them yet.

Few qualified applicants suggests the search is narrow. But without a way to employ someone outside your country, a wider search has nowhere to go. An employer of record is the usual answer. We do not provide one.
