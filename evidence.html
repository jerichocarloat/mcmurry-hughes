Evidence · for employers

Your shortlist isn't the market.

A market map is the research we do for one role: a count of the people who could do that job outside the market the client already searches.

It is not a candidate database or a list of names. It covers one role, it is dated, and it states what we could not measure on the same page. Below is an example map for one role, followed by our own placement record, which is real and counted.

What a marker says

The role the person does now, their years of experience, where they are, their language, what we know and what we do not. Every one ends with not contacted. A marker does not mean someone has applied, is available or is interested.

What it never says

A name, a photograph, a current employer or a link. The map shows that a market exists outside your search. It is not a list of people you can buy.

What the grey block means

The market the client already searches: here, Stuttgart. Grey squares are people inside it. Red squares are people outside it, whom that search would not reach.
