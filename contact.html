Tell us the role · for employers

Show us the role.

Tell us about one role you are finding hard to fill. A named person reads it and replies, usually with one question: can you employ someone who is not local?

If we can take the role on, we send a market snapshot for it: one dated page showing who could do the job outside the market you already search. It is free, it comes before you commit to anything, and you keep it whether or not we work together.

A chat widget, a calendar booking link, a newsletter, or a time-to-hire figure we cannot stand behind.
