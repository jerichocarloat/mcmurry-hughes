Request a market map · for employers

Show us the role.

Tell us about one role you are finding hard to fill. We research the market for it and send you a market map: a short, dated document showing who could do the job outside the market you already search.

We do not charge for the market map, and you keep it whether or not we work together. One question decides whether we can help at all: can you employ someone who is not local?

What happens next
  1. 01We reply, with one question.
  2. 02We send you the market map for that role.
  3. 03If you want to go ahead, we agree the brief: your standard, the pay, the process, who decides.
  4. 04You receive a first shortlist, with who we ruled out and why.
What you will not get from us

A chat widget, a calendar booking link, a newsletter, or a time-to-hire figure we cannot stand behind.
