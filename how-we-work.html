How we work

We recruit outside the market you already search.

McMurry & Hughes is a recruiting company. We work for companies hiring for roles their own market cannot fill well, and we look in the markets outside it.

A recruiter paid per role in one city cannot afford to build a presence in another, so they work the market they already have. We are set up the other way round: standing outside our clients' markets is a condition of how we operate, not extra effort we promise.

One desk, every role you keep needing Ongoing, not per vacancy Germany · Europe · North America You see the market before you commit
01What happens, in orderFive steps, in order

Five steps, from one role to people you can meet.

This is the whole service. There is nothing else behind it.

01You tell us the roleOne role you are struggling to fill, and one question back from us: can you employ someone who is not local? If the answer is no, we say so on the first reply instead of after three weeks of searching.
02We research the marketWe count the people who could do that job in the markets outside the one you normally recruit from: the ones that can still work your hours. We also write down what we could not measure.
03We show you who is out thereYou get that research as a market map: how many people, where they are, what we know about them. Free, and yours whether or not we continue. It sometimes concludes that your own market is deep enough and you do not need us.
04We find and interview themWe contact those people ourselves, in the language the role needs, and judge them against your standard rather than against who happens to be available.
05You meet the strongestYou get summaries written by whoever did the interview, not forwarded CVs, plus the list of who we ruled out and why. You interview and decide.

02Whether we are any use to youNinety seconds

We're useful to a narrow kind of company.

All five need to be true. The last one is not negotiable.

You hire repeatedlyNot one vacancy. A pattern: the same or adjacent roles, over a year or more.
You have a real standardYou would rather leave the role open than hire whoever is available. It usually shows in how long it has been open.
Your own market is small for itLocation, language, sector or pay band narrows the people who could do it to a handful.
You have no recruiting teamOr one recruiter carrying many functions, with hiring run by founders and line managers.
You can employ someone who isn't localThis one is not negotiable. You need a company entity, an employer of record or a contractor framework already in place. We do not provide any of those and cannot work around them.
We are no use to you if
  • · you have to hire locally
  • · you cannot legally employ someone outside your current setup
  • · there is no real, funded role yet
  • · what you actually want is cheaper labour

We would rather say that on a web page than after a month of searching.

If that is you today

Write anyway. We will tell you what would have to change for us to be useful, and that costs you one email.

05What we are notSo nobody has to guess

We are a recruiting company. That is the whole of it.

Not an employerWe do not employ anyone on your behalf. No entity, no employer of record. You employ the person you hire.
Not payrollWe do not run payroll and we do not invoice for anyone's time.
Not immigrationWe do not provide visas, work permits or sponsorship, and we cannot arrange them.
Not a software companyWe use research tools, databases and automation to do this work. We do not sell any of it, and none of it decides who you meet. People do.
Not cheaper labourWe are not an offshoring service. We search a different market, not a discount one. Sometimes it costs less, sometimes it does not; that is never the argument.
Not a job boardNobody on our talent page is for sale, and nothing here is self-serve. Every introduction goes through a person, with the candidate's agreement.
A usual search

A company searches the market it already knows. An agency works that same market faster. You receive a shortlist and take the agency's word for what else was out there.

What we do instead

We research the market outside the one you already search, and you receive the market the shortlist came from, before you commit to anything and whether or not you hire through us.

Where we work

Clients are mostly in Germany and the rest of Europe, some in the United States and Canada. The people we find are wherever they are: usually Europe, usually able to work your hours.
