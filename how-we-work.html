How we work · for employers

We recruit outside the market you already search.

McMurry & Hughes is a recruiting company. We help employers fill roles that their usual search cannot fill, by looking in the markets outside it.

We work as an outside desk: we research and recruit in the cities, countries and language groups our clients do not usually reach, and we can keep working with you across the roles you need to fill again and again.

01What happens, in orderFive steps

Five steps, from one role to people you can meet.

This is the whole service. There is nothing else behind it.

The five steps of the service
01You tell us the roleOne role you are finding hard to fill. A named person replies, and we aim to do it within one working day, with one question: can you employ someone who is not local? If the answer is no, we tell you straight away that we cannot help with this role.
02We research the marketWe count the people who could do the job in markets outside the one you normally recruit from, where they can still work your hours. We also write down what we could not find out.
03We send you a market mapA market map is a short, dated document about one role: how many people could do it, where they are, and what we know and do not know about them. We do not charge for it, and it is yours whether or not we continue. Sometimes it shows that your own market is big enough and you do not need us.
04We find and interview peopleIf you want to go ahead, we contact those people ourselves, in the language the role needs, and judge them against your standard.
05You meet the strongestYou receive a written summary of each person from whoever interviewed them, not a forwarded CV, plus the list of who we ruled out and why. You interview and you decide.
Request a market map We do not quote a time-to-hire, and we do not offer guarantees we cannot keep.
02Who we can helpA short check

We are useful to a narrow kind of company.

All five need to be true. The last one is essential.

We cannot help if
  • you have to hire locally
  • you cannot legally employ someone outside your current setup
  • there is no real, funded role yet
  • what you want is cheaper labour

If that is you today, write to us anyway. We will tell you what would need to change for us to be useful.

The five conditions
01You hire repeatedlyNot a single vacancy: the same or similar roles, over a year or more.
02You have a clear standardYou would rather leave a role open than hire whoever is available. This often shows in how long the role has been open.
03Your own market is small for the roleLocation, language, sector or pay leaves only a few people near you who could do the job.
04You have no large recruiting teamOr one recruiter covering many functions, with hiring run by founders and line managers.
05You can employ someone who is not localThis one is essential. You need a company entity, an employer of record or a contractor arrangement already in place. We do not provide any of these, and we cannot work around them.

04What we are notSo nobody has to guess

We are a recruiting company. That is the whole of it.

A usual search

Many searches, including many run by agencies, stay inside the market the company already knows. You receive a shortlist, but not a view of what else was out there.

What we do instead

We research the market outside the one you already search, and we show you that market before you commit to anything.

Where we work

We recruit for companies in Germany, the rest of Europe, the United States and Canada. The people we find are usually in Europe and able to work your hours.

What McMurry & Hughes does not do
Not an employerWe do not employ anyone on your behalf. We are not an employer of record. You employ the person you hire.
Not payrollWe do not run payroll, and we do not invoice for anyone's time.
Not immigrationWe do not provide or arrange visas, work permits or sponsorship.
Not a software companyWe use research tools and databases to do this work. We do not sell them, and none of them decides who you meet. People do.
Not cheaper labourWe search a different market, not a discount one. Sometimes a hire costs less and sometimes it does not; that is never the reason to use us.
Not a job boardNothing here is self-service. Every introduction goes through a person, with the candidate's agreement.

We'll show you the market before we ask you for anything.
